## Overview

"Learn by Doing – Prompt Engineering 101" offers a structured, interactive approach to learning the fundamentals of prompt engineering with AI. The course centers on an application built to interact with the open-source Mixtral-8x7B model through a ChatGPT-style interface, teaching learners to build effective AI prompts. Hands-on labs rather than passive lecture; aimed at both AI newcomers and professionals improving prompting skills for research, creative work, and programming.

## Course Content

Single module — "Learn by Doing – Prompt Engineering 101" — 8 lessons:

1. Introduction
2. Detailing for Clarity — lab on adding precise detail to prompts
3. Persona Play — lab on crafting AI persona-based prompts
4. Delimiters — lab on using delimiters to organize complex queries
5. Step-by-Step Clarification — lab on breaking tasks into sequential steps
6. Illustrating Through Examples — lab on using examples to guide AI output style/format
7. Specifying Output Length — lab on instructing desired response length
8. How to Reach Out to KodeKloud and Engage with the Community

Plus a closing, ungraded: Feedback

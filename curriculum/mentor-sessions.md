# Mentor Session Guide — 60 Sessions / 20 Weeks

2 groups × 25, 2h sessions, 3×/week. Suggested: same days for both groups (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri), staggered by time of day — one mentor, one live prep.

## Weekly Rhythm (applies to every week below)

| Session                     | Role   | What happens                                                                                                |
| --------------------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **S1 — Kickoff**            | Lead   | Quick group review of last week's HW (2–3 examples), live demo of new topic, assign S2 prep                 |
| **S2 — Practice**           | Coach  | Hands-on lab / pair work on the week's material; mentor circulates, unblocks                                |
| **S3 — Feedback + Stretch** | Review | Extra mentor-set challenge beyond the course's own labs; individual HW review + feedback; preview next week |

**HW feedback rubric (use every S3):** did it run/complete · code quality & readability · did they explain their approach · one thing to improve next time.

Course videos are async pre-work, watched before/between sessions — sessions are for building, reviewing, and troubleshooting, not passive viewing.
